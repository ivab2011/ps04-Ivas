# Remaining manual steps

1. Generate narration audio for each package/video_scripts/NN.md with text-to-speech (Chatterbox, own voice) and combine with a title slide using ffmpeg (3-5 min video).
2. Upload each video to YouTube (public or unlisted); paste the article link and package/sources/NN.md into the description.
3. Publish each package/articles/NN.md on Substack (free access); link the article to its video.
4. Fill substack_url and youtube_url in package/publication_links.json.
