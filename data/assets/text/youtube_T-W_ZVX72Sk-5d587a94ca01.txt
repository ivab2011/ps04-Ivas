A $650 drone can destroy a $4 million tank. Uncrewed boats costing a quarter of a million dollars sink warships worth $65 million. The time between detecting and striking targets has compressed dramatically. A new technology can be deployed in weeks rather than years. The lesson is clear. Military power is no longer measured simply by who has the biggest, most expensive platforms.
It is about who can innovate fastest, produce at scale, and turn relatively cheap technology to devastating military effects.
